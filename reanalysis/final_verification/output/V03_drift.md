Not shown: O/E over ALL calendar-2023+ follow-up of pre-2023 anchors. Much of that follow-up lies more than 5 years after the anchor, beyond the development model's follow-up (censored at 2022-12-31, maximum 5.0 years). Expected counts there would rely on a baseline hazard held flat beyond its estimable range (an artefact, not evidence). The comparison above uses the same 0–3-year window since anchor for both cohorts.

CKD is the only comorbidity whose date was verified from raw extracts (dx files 22–31, first code on or before the anchor). It shows no trend by anchor year, whereas hypertension, CAD and diabetes (master-file flags) fall 6–8% per year after adjustment. This is compatible with those flags partly reflecting codes recorded after the anchor, which accumulate more in older cohorts. It is also compatible with real case-mix change. These data cannot distinguish the two.
