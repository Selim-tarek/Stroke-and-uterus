## 3. Outcome validation [S4a, S4b, S4c]

- **Events are unique women, not encounters.** `stroke_type` holds the single most specific type coded. 98 eligible women had several types coded (e.g. ischemic + ICH), recorded in `notes`.
- **Stroke dates are not chart-verified.** `stroke_date` is the earliest coded diagnosis or problem-list date (Codebook).

**Timing of the 1,538 strokes/TIAs in eligible women:**

| Type | Before index | Same encounter | After index | Undated | Code 9 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ischemic stroke | 541 | 85 | 397 | 166 | 0 | 1,189 |
| TIA | 93 | 6 | 99 | 0 | 0 | 198 |
| ICH | 20 | 3 | 26 | 8 | 0 | 57 |
| SAH | 10 | 0 | 15 | 15 | 0 | 40 |
| CVT | 10 | 1 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 13 |
| Unknown type | 17 | 0 | 11 | 0 | 13 | 41 |
| **All** | **691** | **95** | **550** | **189** | **13** | **1,538** |

- The original reported counts (1,189 / 198 / 57 / 40 / 13 / 41) are **confirmed**. However, **51% of them (786) occurred before or at the index encounter**, and 13% (202) are undated.
- **Code kinds for the 550 strokes after index:**
  - 527 have at least one acute stroke code
  - 10 have only sequela codes ("late effects of stroke", which may mark an old event recorded later)
  - 13 come from imaging alone
  - Sensitivity analysis S5 restricts events to acute-coded strokes.
- **Undated events** (202) are mostly imaging-derived ischemic events (166) and SAH (15). They cannot be placed in time and are excluded from the incident analysis.
- **TIA vs stroke:** TIA is analyzed separately (secondary outcome). Combining them hides a TIA association that is absent (Deliverable C).

## 4. Exposure and laboratory quality [S3c, S3d, S3e]

- **The Hb extract mixes test types.** Of 1,069,076 rows, 915,445 are laboratory CBC Hb and 3,609 point-of-care Hb. The rest are excluded:
  - urine and qualitative Hb (82,147 rows coded 9999999)
  - blood-gas and co-oximetry Hb (median ~10 g/dL, drawn in acute care)
  - HbA1c, Hb fractions, reticulocyte Hb, plasma Hb
  - comment rows

  After unit, sentinel, plausibility (3–20 g/dL) and date checks, **905,494 CBC rows** remain. Every description and its classification is listed in S3c.
- **Validation of the Codebook `hgb`:** re-deriving Hb with the Codebook rule (closest to index within ±3 y, ties to pre-index, POC included) from this independent export gives:
  - the same date for 32,492 of 32,683 women
  - an identical value (±0.05) for 31,842
  - the same anemia classification for **32,588 (99.7%)**
- **Timing of the Codebook Hb:** measured after index in 13,661 women, before index in 12,182 and on the index date in 6,840. Among stroke cases it was after the stroke in 699, before in 483 and the same day in 93, with 263 not determinable. The original cross-sectional exposure therefore often **post-dates the outcome**.
- **Same-day repeated Hb:** 140,793 woman-days have more than one CBC Hb. On 11,326 of them the results span grades, and 5,946 of those involve Hb <8. Severe anemia is largely a feature of hospital days with repeated draws. The R analysis uses the daily median.
- **MCV, platelets and the Codebook `lab_date`:** `lab_date` was back-filled with the D-dimer date (Codebook), so it does not date MCV or platelets. MCV (836,801 rows), RDW-CV (829,620), platelets (857,613) and MCH/MCHC were re-derived from their dated raw extracts and taken from the blood count nearest the baseline Hb (within 30 d, not after index).
  - The platelet unit check initially rejected about 20,000 valid results with equivalent ×10³/µL unit spellings. This was corrected: only % and fL units are rejected.
- **Ferritin:** available only as abstracted values (13,827 eligible women; 54% missing in the incident cohort). Its dates trace to the `hgb_ferritin_update` sheet, but no raw extract was supplied, so ferritin **cannot be traced to source**. It is not used in the revised analysis.
- **Treatment:** transfusion is not captured. Iron and ESA are captured only when administered at the facility (no outpatient oral iron).

## 5. Covariates: timing and quality

| Variable | Timing per Codebook | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidemia | "as of index" (stated); no dated source supplied | Accepted as baseline |
| CAD | Dated on or before index | True baseline |
| Migraine, thrombophilia, AF, heart failure, VTE | Problem list or diagnoses at **any date** | May post-date stroke; thrombophilia is typically diagnosed during a stroke work-up |
| Hormonal therapy, antithrombotics | Window index −365 d **onward** | Partly after index; oral contraceptives under-captured |
| Comorbidities from diagnosis extracts | Dated; R uses on or before index (GI bleeding and pregnancy: year before index) | True baseline |
| Smoking | 73.2% unknown (eligible); 66.2% in the incident cohort | The Codebook itself notes the current-smoker rate is implausibly low. Kept as an "unknown" level, not imputed (likely missing not at random) |
| LDL | Implausible distribution (median 117, P75 188, >190 mg/dL in 7,356) | Not used |
| Race | Free text; "0" in 80 eligible women | Collapsed to 4 groups |

Some diagnosis dates fall after the extraction date (up to December 2026). They never enter baseline flags.

## 6. Missingness and selection [S1a–S1d]

- **Incident cohort:**
  - BMI missing in 880 (3.5%); multiple imputation in the primary analysis, complete case in S1
  - hormonal type missing in 1
  - smoking unknown in 16,461 (66.2%)
  - MCV, RDW and platelets missing in 2.6–3.4%
- **Women with vs without a baseline Hb** (stroke-free at index, n = 24,872 vs 13,455): untested women had far less recorded health care.

  | Characteristic | Untested | Tested |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hypertension | 8.2% | 17.8% |
  | Diabetes | 7.5% | 22.5% |
  | Smoking unknown | 87.1% | 66.2% |
  | Death recorded | 0.1% | 0.7% |
  | Incident stroke (crude rate) | 2.94 per 1,000 PY | 4.16 per 1,000 PY |

  Adjusted for age, race, hypertension, diabetes and dyslipidemia, the rate ratio for being untested was **0.85 (0.69–1.07)**. Testing is clearly not random: untested women are also less observed for both exposure and outcome. S11 re-weights the tested cohort to resemble all stroke-free women on measured characteristics, but no method can correct for unmeasured differences in how intensely women were observed.

## 7. Unresolved discrepancies and data problems

1. 786 of 1,538 strokes (51%) are prevalent at index, and 202 are undated. The original "primary" outcome is mainly prevalent disease.
2. Stroke dates are the earliest coded dates, not chart-verified event dates. 10 after-index events rest on sequela codes only and 13 on imaging only.
3. There is no admission history, so Hb drawn during acute illness cannot be identified directly. Repeated same-day draws are a partial marker.
4. Ferritin cannot be traced to a raw extract.
5. Undated covariates (thrombophilia, AF, heart failure, VTE, migraine) and post-index medication windows (hormonal therapy, antithrombotics) limit adjustment in an incident design.
6. Index date = first diagnosis within an extract that starts on 2018-01-01. Women diagnosed before 2018 have a left-truncated index.
7. Codebook `study_id` is documented as "STK-nnnn" but is numeric in the file (cosmetic).
