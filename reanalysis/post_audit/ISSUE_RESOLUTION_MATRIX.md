## Chart-review protocol for row 2 (to run only if chart access is granted)

- **Sample:** the 100 events in `derived/audit/chart_review_sample_events.csv`.
  - Stratum A: all 58 events that are non-acute, imaging-only or within 30 days of the anchor; weight 1.
  - Stratum B: 42 events drawn at random from the other 287; weight 287/42.
  - The frame was built on the published development sample. The baseline cohort's acute-coded events are a subset of it.
- **Case definition:**
  - Acute stroke: an acute focal neurological deficit with imaging or clinical diagnosis of ischemic stroke, ICH, SAH or CVT (AHA/ASA 2013 tissue-based definition for ischemic stroke). Report the subtype.
  - Not a case: TIA; a historical stroke recorded at a later encounter; incidental chronic infarct without a clinical event; or a rule-out diagnosis.
  - Date the event as the symptom onset or admission date. Record whether it falls before or after the anchor.
- **Blinding:** reviewers see only the clinical documents within ±60 days of the coded date. They do not see predictor values, model predictions, or which stratum the event came from.
- **Process:** two independent reviewers (neurology), each with a standardized form; κ reported; disagreements resolved by a third reviewer.
- **Estimation:**
  - PPV estimated as the stratum-weighted proportion confirmed. CI from the stratified (design-based) variance, or Wilson intervals combined with stratum weights.
  - Report it separately for acute-coded events, events within 30 days, and imaging-only events.
  - Also report whether each confirmed event is incident, i.e. after the anchor.
- **What it cannot estimate:**
  - Sensitivity: missed strokes would need a sample of non-events (about 1.3% 3-year risk). Detecting misses with useful precision needs thousands of reviews or linkage to an independent registry.
  - Specificity: near 1 by construction, and uninformative.
